# Motor Insurance Policy Rules

> Source: AI Engineer Assignment PDF §5.1 (verbatim content, reformatted
> with explicit clause IDs so the triage agent can cite them).
> Policy version: v1.0, effective 2026-01-01.

---

## Section 1 — Covered Events

### Clause 1.1 — Covered Perils

The policy covers the following events:

- Accidental collision
- Theft
- Fire
- Flood
- Third-party property damage

---

## Section 2 — Exclusions

### Clause 2.1 — Alcohol or Drug Impairment

Damage caused while the insured is driving under the influence of alcohol
or drugs is not covered.

### Clause 2.2 — Illegal Racing

Damage arising from participation in illegal racing is not covered.

### Clause 2.3 — Intentional Damage

Intentional damage caused by the insured or an authorised driver is not
covered.

### Clause 2.4 — Geographic Coverage

Use of the vehicle outside the permitted geographic coverage area is not
covered.

### Clause 2.5 — Late Submission

Claims filed more than 30 days after the incident without a valid reason
are not covered. If a valid reason is provided, the claim may be
considered through manual review.

---

## Section 3 — Required Documents

### Clause 3.1 — Standard Required Documents

For all claims the following documents must be submitted:

- Claim form
- Copy of driving license
- Vehicle registration
- Photos of damage
- Incident report

### Clause 3.2 — Additional Documents for Theft

For theft claims, in addition to Clause 3.1, the following is required:

- Police report

### Clause 3.3 — Additional Documents for Third-Party Damage

For third-party damage claims, in addition to Clause 3.1, the following
is required:

- Third-party contact information
- Third-party evidence (e.g., photos, statement)

---

## Section 4 — Routing Rules

### Clause 4.1 — Standard Processing

Claims are routed to standard processing when all of the following hold:

- The incident is a clear covered event (Clause 1.1)
- All required documents (Section 3) are present
- No risk flags are detected

### Clause 4.2 — Manual Review

Claims are routed to manual review when any of the following hold:

- Coverage is unclear
- Required documents are incomplete
- There is conflicting information in the submission

### Clause 4.3 — Fraud Review

Claims are routed to fraud review when one or more of the following
suspicious patterns are detected:

- Inconsistent story
- Repeated claims from the same customer over a short period
- Severe damage reported with weak or missing supporting evidence

### Clause 4.4 — Rejection Review

Claims are routed to rejection review when a clear exclusion (Section 2)
applies.
